# Generic Notice (free-form)

> **NON-STANDARD — FULL LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.** This type has no approved wording at
> all. It exists so a notice that does not fit any of the other types can still be put on
> CEAT's letterhead in CEAT's house style, with the structure, numbering and signature
> block right, instead of being written from nothing in Word.
>
> Every other type in this app asserts something specific and is checked against a
> type-specific checklist. This one asserts whatever the drafter types. **It is a drafting
> aid for a lawyer, not a notice that can be issued on the strength of the app's checks.**
> The draft says so on its face and that marking is not removable.

## When to use
The matter is a genuine outgoing notice from CEAT but it is not a cheque dishonour, a
recovery, a consumer reply, a breach, a termination, a renewal, a force majeure, a price
revision or a cease and desist. For example: a notice of set-off, a notice invoking an
arbitration clause, a notice calling for inspection or audit under a contract, a notice
rejecting a claim, a notice of appropriation of a security deposit, a reply to a
non-consumer legal notice, a notice withdrawing a credit facility.

If the matter does fit one of the other types, use that type. Those carry real checklists,
type-specific validation and approved or structured wording. Choosing this tab to avoid
answering the questions another type asks is the one way to misuse it.

## When NOT to use
- Anything with a statutory form or a statutory period — in particular a Section 138
  demand, which has a 15-day period and a 30-day window that this type does not check.
- Anything where the other types' arithmetic matters: a money demand with interest, a
  statement of account, a part-payment calculation.

## Required inputs (ask for any that are missing)
- What this notice is called (used in the title and the subject line).
- Other party: individual/proprietor OR company + directors OR partnership + partners;
  name(s) and address(es).
- The facts the notice rests on.
- What the Company requires the other side to do.
- The period or date by which it must be done.
- What the Company will do if it is not done.
- Place of jurisdiction, if proceedings are contemplated.
- Signatory, and confirmation that they are authorised to sign a notice of this kind.

## Template
```
{{LETTERHEAD}}

{{DATE}}
THROUGH SPEED POST / EMAIL / WHATSAPP
WITHOUT PREJUDICE

To,
{{NOTICEE_BLOCK}}

Sub: {{SUBJECT}}

Sir/Madam,

1. CEAT Limited ("the Company") is a Public Limited Company incorporated under the
   Companies Act, 1956, having its Registered Office at 463, Dr. Annie Besant Road,
   Worli, Mumbai – 400030, engaged in the manufacture and sale of Tyres, Tubes and Flaps.

2. {{FACTS}}

3. In the aforesaid circumstances, the Company hereby calls upon you to {{DEMAND}}, within
   {{DEADLINE}} from the date of receipt of this notice.

4. Should you fail to comply, the Company shall be constrained to {{CONSEQUENCES}},
   entirely at your risk as to the costs and consequences arising therefrom.

5. This notice is issued without prejudice to the Company's rights and remedies, all of
   which are expressly reserved.

{{SIGNATORY_BLOCK}}
```

## Mandatory checklist (run before delivering)
- [ ] A subject line that says what the notice is.
- [ ] Noticee named and addressed correctly for its constitution (a proprietorship is not
      a separate legal person; a company's directors are named where liability is joint).
- [ ] The facts stated.
- [ ] What is required of the other side stated, in terms that can be complied with.
- [ ] A period or date, running from receipt.
- [ ] Consequences of non-compliance stated.
- [ ] Rights-reserved / without-prejudice line present.
- [ ] `⚠ [VERIFY: everything. This type has no approved wording and no type-specific
      checks. A lawyer must read the whole notice before it is issued.]`
- [ ] Draft marked "NON-STANDARD — full legal review required"; review banner appended.

## Letterhead (same as the approved notices)
Every draft MUST open with CEAT's full official letterhead exactly as defined in
`house-style.md`. The `{{LETTERHEAD}}` placeholder in the template above must be filled
with that block; never produce a notice on a plain or generic header.
