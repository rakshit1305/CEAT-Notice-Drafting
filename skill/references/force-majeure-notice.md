# Force Majeure Notice

> **NON-STANDARD — no CEAT sample provided yet.** Built from standard legal structure +
> CEAT house style. Draft output must be marked "NON-STANDARD — for legal review" and
> validated by CEAT's legal team. Replace with CEAT's approved wording once samples exist.

## When to use
An event beyond CEAT's control (or the other party's) prevents or delays performance of an
agreement, and CEAT wants to formally invoke the force majeure clause — to excuse/suspend
its own performance, or to respond to the other party's claimed force majeure.

## Required inputs (ask for any that are missing)
- Direction: CEAT is invoking FM, OR CEAT is responding to the other party's FM claim
- Other party: name(s), type, address(es)
- Agreement: title, date, and the force majeure clause number
- The force majeure event and the date it began
- The affected obligations and the expected impact/duration
- Any notice timeline required by the clause
- Mitigation steps being taken; what CEAT asks for (suspension of timelines, etc.)
- Signatory

## Template
```
{{LETTERHEAD}}

{{DATE}}
THROUGH SPEED POST / EMAIL / WHATSAPP
WITHOUT PREJUDICE

To,
{{NOTICEE_BLOCK}}

Sub: Notice invoking Force Majeure under {{AGREEMENT_NAME}} dated {{AGREEMENT_DATE}}.

Sir/Madam,

1. The Company and you are parties to {{AGREEMENT_NAME}} dated {{AGREEMENT_DATE}} ("the
   Agreement"), which contains a force majeure provision at Clause {{CLAUSE_NO}}.

2. On/from {{FM_EVENT_DATE}}, {{FM_EVENT_DESCRIPTION}} has occurred, being an event beyond
   the Company's reasonable control within the meaning of Clause {{CLAUSE_NO}}.

3. As a direct consequence, the Company is prevented/delayed from performing
   {{AFFECTED_OBLIGATIONS}}. The expected impact/duration is {{IMPACT_DURATION}}.

4. The Company is taking the following steps to mitigate the effect of the said event:
   {{MITIGATION_STEPS}}.

5. Accordingly, in terms of Clause {{CLAUSE_NO}}, the Company hereby invokes force majeure
   and calls upon you to treat the affected obligations as suspended/excused for the
   duration of the event, and to {{RELIEF_SOUGHT}} (e.g., extend the timelines
   accordingly). The Company will keep you informed and resume performance as soon as
   reasonably practicable.

6. This notice is without prejudice to the Company's rights and remedies under the
   Agreement and in law, all of which are expressly reserved.

{{SIGNATORY_BLOCK}}
```

## Mandatory checklist (run before delivering)
- [ ] Force majeure clause number cited.
- [ ] The force majeure event and its start date clearly stated.
- [ ] Affected obligations and expected impact/duration stated.
- [ ] Mitigation steps stated (clauses often require reasonable-endeavours mitigation).
- [ ] Relief sought (suspension/extension of timelines) stated.
- [ ] Rights-reserved / without-prejudice line present.
- [ ] `⚠ [VERIFY: force majeure clause number, whether the event falls within its
      definition, and the notice deadline the clause imposes — FM claims are frequently
      lost for late or non-conforming notice.]`
- [ ] Draft marked "NON-STANDARD — for legal review"; review banner appended.
