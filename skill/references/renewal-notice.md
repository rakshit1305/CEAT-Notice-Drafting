# Renewal / Non-Renewal Notice

> **NON-STANDARD — no CEAT sample provided yet.** Built from standard legal structure +
> CEAT house style. Draft output must be marked "NON-STANDARD — for legal review" and
> validated by CEAT's legal team. Replace with CEAT's approved wording once samples exist.

## When to use
An agreement is approaching expiry and CEAT wants to formally notify the other party
either that it intends to renew (and on what terms), or that it will NOT renew and the
agreement will end on expiry. Select the correct variant.

## Required inputs (ask for any that are missing)
- Intent: RENEW or NOT RENEW
- Other party: individual/proprietor OR company + directors; name(s) and address(es)
- Agreement: title, date, and expiry date
- The renewal/notice clause number
- If renewing: proposed renewal term and any revised terms; deadline to confirm
- If not renewing: the required notice period; confirmation the agreement ends on expiry
- Wind-down items (if not renewing): dues, return of stock/property, trademark cessation
- Signatory

## Template
```
{{LETTERHEAD}}

{{DATE}}
THROUGH SPEED POST / EMAIL / WHATSAPP
WITHOUT PREJUDICE

To,
{{NOTICEE_BLOCK}}

Sub: {{Notice of Renewal / Notice of Non-Renewal}} of {{AGREEMENT_NAME}} dated
{{AGREEMENT_DATE}}.

Sir/Madam,

1. CEAT Limited ("the Company") and you are parties to {{AGREEMENT_NAME}} dated
   {{AGREEMENT_DATE}} ("the Agreement"), which is due to expire on {{EXPIRY_DATE}}.

2. {{RENEWAL_OR_NON_RENEWAL_PARAGRAPH}}
   (Renew: "In terms of Clause {{CLAUSE_NO}}, the Company hereby conveys its intention to
   renew the Agreement for a further term of {{RENEWAL_TERM}} on {{the existing / the
   following revised}} terms: {{REVISED_TERMS}}. Kindly confirm your acceptance on or
   before {{CONFIRM_BY_DATE}}."
   Not renew: "In terms of Clause {{CLAUSE_NO}}, the Company hereby gives notice that it
   does NOT intend to renew the Agreement, which shall accordingly stand expired/terminated
   on {{EXPIRY_DATE}} without further notice.")

3. {{WIND_DOWN_PARAGRAPH — for non-renewal}}
   (Upon expiry, you are called upon to clear outstanding dues of INR {{AMOUNT}}, return
   Company property/stock, and cease all use of the Company's trademarks and materials.)

4. This notice is without prejudice to the Company's rights and remedies, all of which
   are expressly reserved.

{{SIGNATORY_BLOCK}}
```

## Mandatory checklist (run before delivering)
- [ ] Correct variant chosen (renewal vs non-renewal) and reflected in subject and body.
- [ ] Agreement identified by name, date, AND expiry date.
- [ ] Renewal/notice clause cited.
- [ ] Renewal: renewal term, any revised terms, and a confirm-by date stated.
- [ ] Non-renewal: required notice period honoured; end-on-expiry stated; wind-down listed.
- [ ] Rights-reserved / without-prejudice line present.
- [ ] `⚠ [VERIFY: renewal/notice clause number and the required notice period against the
      actual signed agreement — non-renewal notices are often time-barred if sent late.]`
- [ ] Draft marked "NON-STANDARD — for legal review"; review banner appended.
