# Clause Library — shared approved blocks

Reusable, pre-approved paragraph blocks common across CEAT's notices. Use verbatim;
adapt only the bracketed facts. Do not rewrite the legal language.

## Company introduction (outbound demand notices)
> We, CEAT Limited, having our Registered Office at 463, Dr. Annie Besant Road, Worli,
> Mumbai – 400030, are a Public Limited Company duly incorporated/registered under the
> provisions of the Companies Act, 1956. We deal in the business of manufacture and sale
> of Tyres, Tubes and Flaps (hereinafter referred to as the "{{GOODS_LABEL}}").
>
> (Use "Goods" in Section 138 notices, "Said Materials" in recovery notices, "Products"
> in consumer replies — matching the sample wording for each type.)

## Noticee — individual / sole proprietor
> That you are the sole proprietor and the person in control and management of the
> proprietorship concern {{FIRM_NAME}}, having your office and residential address at
> {{ADDRESS}}, engaged in the business of purchase and sale of Goods.

## Noticee — company + directors
> That you Noticee No. 1, {{COMPANY_NAME}}, are a company registered in India engaged in
> {{BUSINESS}}; and Noticee Nos. 2 and 3, {{DIRECTOR_1}} and {{DIRECTOR_2}}, are its
> directors responsible for its management and day-to-day operations. (Liability of the
> directors is asserted under Section 141 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, where
> applicable.)

## Acknowledged supply
> That in the course of business and against orders placed by you, the Company sold,
> supplied and delivered the said {{GOODS_LABEL}} from time to time as per your
> requirements, which were duly received, acknowledged and accepted by you without demur
> as to quality and/or quantity thereof.

## Fraud / cheating (BNS) block
> Your conduct reveals an intention tainted with fraud. By deliberately {{issuing the
> dishonoured cheque / accepting the Said Materials and avoiding payment}} with knowledge
> of the consequences, you have deceived and cheated the Company, attracting the penal
> provisions of Section 316(2) (criminal breach of trust) and Section 318(4) (cheating)
> of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 ("BNS"), causing wrongful loss to the Company and
> wrongful gain to yourself.
>
> (Section 138 notices additionally cite the offence under Section 138 of the Negotiable
> Instruments Act, 1881. Recovery notices may additionally reference Sections 2(36) and
> 2(37) of the BNS where used.)

## Retention note (Section 138)
> Please note that a copy of this notice has been retained by us for future reference.

## Prior notice reference (repeat cheque / breach followed by termination)
Use when a case-specific input confirms CEAT has already sent a related notice on this
matter, and the current notice's demand must stay confined to the current
cheque/breach only.
> This notice pertains solely to {{CURRENT_INSTRUMENT_OR_BREACH}} and the amount/relief
> claimed herein does not include {{PRIOR_INSTRUMENT_OR_BREACH}}, which is the subject of
> our separate notice dated {{PRIOR_NOTICE_DATE}}. Nothing in this notice affects the
> Company's rights in respect of that separate matter, all of which are expressly
> reserved.
>
> (Do not use this block to imply the two matters are resolved or connected beyond what
> the user has stated. If the user hasn't confirmed the prior notice's current status —
> served, expired, complaint filed — flag that to the lawyer in chat rather than
> asserting a status here.)

## Jurisdiction / place of proceedings
Use where the case facts include a place of jurisdiction, or default per the reference
file for that notice type.
> Without prejudice to the Company's other rights, any legal proceedings arising out of
> or in connection with this notice shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts at
> {{JURISDICTION_PLACE}}.

## Amount breakup (principal + tax)
Use in Recovery notices where invoices are tax-inclusive and a breakup was supplied.
> The aforesaid outstanding amount of INR {{AMOUNT_FIGURES}} comprises a principal sum of
> INR {{PRINCIPAL_AMOUNT}} and applicable GST/tax of INR {{TAX_AMOUNT}}, as reflected in
> the invoices referred to above.
