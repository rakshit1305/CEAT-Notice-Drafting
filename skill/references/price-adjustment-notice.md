# Price Adjustment / Price Revision Notice

> **NON-STANDARD — no CEAT sample provided yet.** Built from standard legal structure +
> CEAT house style. Draft output must be marked "NON-STANDARD — for legal review" and
> validated by CEAT's legal team. Replace with CEAT's approved wording once samples exist.

## When to use
CEAT wants to formally notify a dealer/customer of a revision in prices of the Goods
(upward or downward), typically under a price-variation clause in the supply/dealership
arrangement, stating the revised prices and the effective date.

## Required inputs (ask for any that are missing)
- Other party: individual/proprietor OR company + directors; name(s) and address(es)
- Agreement / arrangement: title and date (if any), and the price-variation clause number
- Reason for the revision (e.g., input cost/raw-material movement) — brief
- The revised prices (product/SKU-wise) or the % change, and the old vs new reference
- Effective date of the revised prices
- Treatment of orders already placed / in transit before the effective date
- Signatory

## Template
```
{{LETTERHEAD}}

{{DATE}}
THROUGH SPEED POST / EMAIL / WHATSAPP
WITHOUT PREJUDICE

To,
{{NOTICEE_BLOCK}}

Sub: Notice of Price Revision effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

Sir/Madam,

1. CEAT Limited ("the Company") supplies its Tyres, Tubes and Flaps ("Goods") to you
   under {{AGREEMENT_NAME_OR_ARRANGEMENT}} {{dated AGREEMENT_DATE, if applicable}}.

2. On account of {{REASON_FOR_REVISION}}, and in terms of {{Clause CLAUSE_NO / the
   Company's right to revise prices}}, the Company hereby gives notice of a revision in
   the prices of the Goods with effect from {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

3. The revised prices are as follows:
   {{PRICE_TABLE}}   (Product/SKU | Existing Price | Revised Price | % change)

4. Orders {{placed and accepted / dispatched}} before {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} shall be
   {{governed by the existing prices / treated as follows: TREATMENT}}. All orders on or
   after {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} shall be at the revised prices.

5. All other terms and conditions of supply remain unchanged. This notice is without
   prejudice to the Company's rights, all of which are expressly reserved.

{{SIGNATORY_BLOCK}}
```

## Mandatory checklist (run before delivering)
- [ ] Basis for revision stated (price-variation clause number or the reserved right).
- [ ] Revised prices stated clearly (table or % change), with old vs new.
- [ ] A single, unambiguous effective date given.
- [ ] Treatment of pre-existing / in-transit orders addressed.
- [ ] "All other terms unchanged" + rights-reserved line present.
- [ ] `⚠ [VERIFY: the price-variation clause number and any required notice period for a
      price change against the actual signed arrangement.]`
- [ ] Draft marked "NON-STANDARD — for legal review"; review banner appended.
