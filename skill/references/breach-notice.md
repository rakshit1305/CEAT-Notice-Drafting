# Breach / Default Notice

> **NON-STANDARD — no CEAT sample provided yet.** Built from standard legal structure +
> CEAT house style. Draft output must be marked "NON-STANDARD — for legal review" and
> validated by CEAT's legal team. Replace with CEAT's approved wording once samples exist.

## When to use
The other party has breached an obligation under a subsisting agreement and CEAT wants to
formally record the breach and call on them to remedy it (a "cure notice"), reserving the
right to terminate or take further action if they do not.

## Required inputs (ask for any that are missing)
- Other party: individual/proprietor OR company + directors; name(s) and address(es)
- Agreement: title and date; the clause(s) breached
- The specific obligation breached and the factual particulars of the breach
- The cure period being allowed (e.g., 15 days)
- Consequences if not cured (termination / legal proceedings / recovery)
- Signatory
- Whether a related notice on this same agreement already exists (e.g., is this the
  first cure notice, or does the user's description imply a cure notice was already sent
  earlier?). If the facts given here are inconsistent with something said in an earlier
  conversation about the same counterparty and agreement (e.g., this message describes
  "wanting to offer a cure" while an earlier message described a cure notice as already
  sent), surface that contradiction to the user and ask which is accurate before
  drafting either document — don't silently pick one version of events.

## Template
```
{{LETTERHEAD}}

{{DATE}}
THROUGH SPEED POST / EMAIL / WHATSAPP
WITHOUT PREJUDICE

To,
{{NOTICEE_BLOCK}}

Sub: Notice of Breach of {{AGREEMENT_NAME}} dated {{AGREEMENT_DATE}} — call to cure.

Sir/Madam,

1. CEAT Limited ("the Company") is a Public Limited Company incorporated under the
   Companies Act, 1956, having its Registered Office at 463, Dr. Annie Besant Road,
   Worli, Mumbai – 400030, engaged in the manufacture and sale of Tyres, Tubes and Flaps.

2. The Company and you entered into {{AGREEMENT_NAME}} dated {{AGREEMENT_DATE}} ("the
   Agreement").

3. In terms of Clause {{CLAUSE_NO}} of the Agreement, you were obliged to
   {{OBLIGATION}}.

4. You are in breach of the said obligation, in that {{BREACH_FACTS}}. Despite the
   Company's follow-ups, the breach subsists.

5. You are hereby called upon to remedy/cure the aforesaid breach within {{CURE_PERIOD}}
   from the date of receipt of this notice.

6. Should you fail to cure the breach within the said period, the Company shall be
   constrained to {{CONSEQUENCES}} (e.g., terminate the Agreement and/or initiate
   appropriate legal proceedings for recovery of dues and losses), at your entire risk as
   to costs and consequences.

7. This notice is issued without prejudice to the Company's rights and remedies, all of
   which are expressly reserved.

{{SIGNATORY_BLOCK}}
```

## Mandatory checklist (run before delivering)
- [ ] Agreement identified by name AND date; breached clause cited.
- [ ] Specific obligation and factual particulars of the breach stated.
- [ ] A clear cure period given, running from receipt.
- [ ] Consequences of non-cure stated.
- [ ] Rights-reserved / without-prejudice line present.
- [ ] `⚠ [VERIFY: breached clause number and any contractual cure period/notice
      requirement against the actual signed agreement.]`
