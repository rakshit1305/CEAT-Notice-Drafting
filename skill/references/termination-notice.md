# Termination Notice

> **NON-STANDARD — no CEAT sample provided yet.** Built from standard legal structure +
> CEAT house style. Draft output must be marked "NON-STANDARD — for legal review" and
> validated by CEAT's legal team. When CEAT provides real samples, replace this wording
> with their approved language and remove the non-standard marking.

## When to use
CEAT wants to formally end an existing agreement (dealership, supply, service, etc.) —
either for the other party's breach, or as a contractual termination for convenience
under the agreement's own termination clause.

## If the request also involves unpaid dues
A request like "this dealer hasn't paid us, draft something to end the relationship"
usually needs both a Recovery notice and a Termination (or Breach/cure) notice — not one
document that both demands payment and terminates. Bundling a payment demand with a
termination in a single notice tends to undercut both: it invites the argument that no
genuine opportunity to pay or cure was actually given before the relationship was ended.
Ask the user to confirm whether they want one instrument or both, and if both, whether
they should be dated the same day or staggered (e.g., cure/demand first, termination
only if that lapses).

## Required inputs (ask for any that are missing)
- Other party: individual/proprietor OR company + directors; name(s) and address(es)
- Agreement being terminated: title and date
- The clause number that permits termination
- Ground: breach / convenience / expiry
- If breach-based: the obligation breached, the facts, and whether a cure period was given
- Notice period required by the agreement (for termination for convenience)
- Effective date of termination
- Wind-down items: outstanding dues (amount), return of stock/property, cessation of
  trademark use, confidentiality survival
- Signatory
- If breach-based and a cure period already lapsed: the date it lapsed. Check this
  against today's date — a long gap between the cure deadline and this termination
  notice is a real risk (the other party can argue CEAT continued dealing with them and
  so affirmed/waived the right to terminate on that breach); flag this to the lawyer
  rather than deciding it, and suggest they consider whether a fresh cure notice is
  needed first.
- If this termination follows an earlier breach/cure notice on the same agreement,
  confirm the facts of that earlier notice are consistent with what was said about it
  previously in this conversation (date sent, cure period, whether it lapsed) — if the
  two descriptions conflict, ask which is accurate before drafting either document.

## Template
```
{{LETTERHEAD}}

{{DATE}}
THROUGH SPEED POST / EMAIL / WHATSAPP
WITHOUT PREJUDICE

To,
{{NOTICEE_BLOCK}}

Sub: Notice of Termination of {{AGREEMENT_NAME}} dated {{AGREEMENT_DATE}}.

Sir/Madam,

1. CEAT Limited ("the Company") is a Public Limited Company incorporated under the
   Companies Act, 1956, having its Registered Office at 463, Dr. Annie Besant Road,
   Worli, Mumbai – 400030, engaged in the manufacture and sale of Tyres, Tubes and Flaps.

2. The Company and you entered into {{AGREEMENT_NAME}} dated {{AGREEMENT_DATE}} ("the
   Agreement"), governing {{SUBJECT_OF_AGREEMENT}}.

3. {{GROUND_PARAGRAPH}}
   (Breach: "In terms of Clause {{CLAUSE_NO}}, you were required to {{OBLIGATION}}. You
   have failed to do so, as {{FACTS}}, despite {{notice/cure period given on DATE}}."
   Convenience: "In terms of Clause {{CLAUSE_NO}}, the Company is entitled to terminate
   the Agreement by giving {{NOTICE_PERIOD}} notice.")

4. Accordingly, the Company hereby gives notice of termination of the Agreement under
   Clause {{CLAUSE_NO}}, with effect from {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

5. Upon termination, you are called upon to {{WIND_DOWN_OBLIGATIONS}} (e.g., clear all
   outstanding dues of INR {{AMOUNT}}, return Company property/stock, and cease all use
   of the Company's trademarks, signage and materials).

6. This termination is without prejudice to the Company's rights and remedies, all of
   which are expressly reserved, including for recovery of dues and losses.

{{SIGNATORY_BLOCK}}
```

## Mandatory checklist (run before delivering)
- [ ] Agreement identified by name AND date.
- [ ] Termination clause number cited.
- [ ] Ground clearly stated (breach / convenience / expiry).
- [ ] For breach: the breached obligation and facts stated; cure period referenced if given.
- [ ] Effective date of termination given.
- [ ] Rights-reserved / without-prejudice line present.
- [ ] `⚠ [VERIFY: termination clause number, required notice period, and any cure
      requirement against the actual signed agreement.]`
