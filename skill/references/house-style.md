## Standard mode-of-sending line
`BY SPEED POST` or `THROUGH SPEED POST / EMAIL / WHATSAPP` (as instructed for the case).
Always capture this from the user rather than defaulting silently — many statutory
timelines run from the date of *service*, not the date of drafting, so the mode matters
beyond just the wording of this line.

## File naming
Name every delivered .docx `CEAT_<NoticeType>_<CounterpartyName>_<YYYYMMDD>.docx` (e.g.
`CEAT_Section138_Notice_Ramesh_Kumar_20260906.docx`). For a fill-in/specimen document
produced under the narrow exception in `SKILL.md` Step 5a, append `_FILL-IN` or
`_SPECIMEN` so it's never mistaken for a ready-to-send draft by filename alone.

## Signatory authority
Before inserting the signatory block below, confirm with the user that the named person
is currently authorised to sign a notice of this type — don't assume the default
signatory shown here is always the right one, particularly for higher-stakes notices
(Termination, Breach) where signing authority may sit with a specific role rather than
whoever is named in an old sample.

## "WITHOUT PREJUDICE"
Include immediately below the mode line on outbound demand notices, as in the samples.

## Noticee block
- **Individual/proprietor:** single addressee (name, then address).
- **Company:** number the noticees — Noticee No. 1 (the company), Noticee No. 2 & 3
  (directors) — and use joint-and-several language in the demand.

(Or the authorised signatory named for the case, e.g., "Sr. Manager". Confirm the correct
signatory and designation per notice.)

## Formatting conventions (observed in the samples)
- Amounts written in figures **and** words: `INR 74,271.88/- (Rupees Seventy Four
  Thousand Two Hundred Seventy Two Only)`.
- Dates in `DD.MM.YYYY`.
- Numbered paragraphs for the body.
- Demand period spelled out: `15 (FIFTEEN) days`, `10 days`.

## Trusted legal sources (whitelist — for verifying a provision when needed)
Use ONLY these to check a statute/section/notification. Never rely on open-internet
search for legal content, and never state a provision that cannot be traced to one of
these (or to an approved template). If a source is not accessible, flag `⚠` rather than
guessing.

**Official (primary):**
- India Code — indiacode.nic.in (central & state bare Acts, with amendments)
- eGazette — egazette.gov.in (official notifications)
- Ministry of Corporate Affairs — mca.gov.in
- Ministry of Consumer Affairs — consumeraffairs.nic.in; NCDRC — ncdrc.nic.in

**Case law (interpretation):**
- Supreme Court — sci.gov.in; eCourts — ecourts.gov.in
- Indian Kanoon — indiankanoon.org (reference only; verify anything critical)

**Paid databases (CEAT-subscribed / professional):**
- Manupatra (login-based — CEAT's stated source of truth), SCC Online, LexisNexis India

CEAT's legal team should confirm and finalise this list; they may standardise on specific
sources (Manupatra was named as their primary in the scope discussion).
